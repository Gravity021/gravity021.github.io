Hi, I’m Isaac, a Computer Systems Engineering student at the University of Warwick.

I’m interested in the space where hardware meets software, and I like understanding how things work from the circuit level up. So far that has meant writing C for an STM32 microcontroller to drive a 3D Snake game on a custom 8x8x8 LED array, building a movie database in Java with my own hash maps and search trees, and exploring GPU architecture and rendering pipelines by developing a game engine with OpenGL.

Away from my degree, I’m a lighting designer with Warwick Tech Crew and a casual technician at Warwick Arts Centre, which has taught me a lot about working under pressure and communicating clearly as part of a team. I also manage a premium hospitality lounge for Coventry City Football Club.

In my spare time, you'll find me mountain biking, climbing and playing cornet in various bands.

Have a look at my [projects](/projects/), or read my [CV](/cv/) for more detail.
